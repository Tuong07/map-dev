Sensor tests

Phase 0. Each page answers one question. None of this ships — only the tuned numbers do.

1 · Raw sensorsCan the phone read motion at all, and how fast? 2 · Step counterDoes it count my steps within 5%? 3 · Turn detectorDoes it know left from right? 4 · QR scan timerDo codes scan fast in a dim hallway? 5 · Fake corridorDoes the dot follow me? Go / no-go. R · RecorderLog a walk to a file so tuning happens at a desk.

Order

Run 1 at your desk first — if it fails, nothing else matters.

Then use the recorder for one walk before tuning anything. Tests 2 and 3 can be replayed from that file with analyze.mjs instead of walking again.

4 and 5 need to happen in Wheatley itself.

Posture

Hold the phone flat, screen up, like you're following directions. Yaw only equals your walking direction in that posture. In a pocket or swinging at your side, the numbers are meaningless — that's a known limit, not a bug.
