    How to use it

    1. Name the walk, hit start.
    2. Hold the phone flat and walk your route, counting steps out loud.
    3. Tap Mark corner the moment you turn.
    4. Stop, download, AirDrop the file to your Mac.
    5. node probes/analyze.mjs walk-01.json

    Once the page has loaded, it keeps working with no signal — safe in basements and stairwells. Just don't reload down there.
