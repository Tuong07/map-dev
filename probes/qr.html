Test matrix

Print the same code at 5 cm, 10 cm, and 15 cm. For each size, try:

· straight on at 1 m
· at roughly 45°
· from 2 m
· walking past without stopping

The smallest size that decodes in under 2 s from 1 m at 45° is the one you print fifteen of. Getting this wrong means re-taping every sticker later.

Payload format

Keep it short — fewer characters means a coarser, easier-to-read code. Encode only an anchor id like W2-J07, not a full URL.
