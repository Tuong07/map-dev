    What passes

    Walk a rectangle that returns you to where you started, holding the phone flat.

    Pass: 4 turns detected, every direction correct, total within about ±30° of 360.
    Fail: a missed turn, or any direction reported backwards.

    Being off by 20° on an individual angle is fine — the real app snaps heading to the hallway's true bearing at every junction, so small errors get wiped out.
